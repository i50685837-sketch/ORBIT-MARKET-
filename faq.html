❓ Orbit Market FAQ

What is Orbit Market? +
Orbit Market is a platform interface designed for investment tracking, market information, and trading features.
How do I create an account? +
Click Register, provide your details, verify your account, then log in to access your dashboard.
How can I deposit funds? +
Go to the Wallet section and select Deposit. Follow the available payment instructions.
How do withdrawals work? +
Submit a withdrawal request from your wallet. The request will be processed according to platform rules.
Can I trade Forex? +
Yes, the Forex section provides access to market information and trading features.
How do I contact support? +
Visit the Contact page or Support section to reach the Orbit Market team.
